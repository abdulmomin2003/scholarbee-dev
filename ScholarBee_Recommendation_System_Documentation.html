In Simple Terms: Instead of showing every student the same generic list of programs, ScholarBee now shows each student a personally curated list — sorted by how well each program matches what they told us they want (their degree goal, preferred cities, budget, fields of interest) and what they've been browsing on the platform.

Key Benefits

  • Higher Student Engagement — Students find relevant programs faster, reducing drop-off
  • Increased Applications — Matching programs to student preferences drives more applications
  • Partner University Visibility — ScholarBee-verified partners receive a built-in ranking boost
  • Zero-Effort Personalization — Works automatically after the student completes onboarding
  • Smarter Over Time — The system learns from student behavior to improve recommendations

🔄 2. How It Works — The Big Picture

The recommendation system follows a clear pipeline from student input to personalized output. Here's the entire flow at a glance.

💡 Key Insight: The system works even for anonymous visitors (not logged in). In that case, it falls back to showing the most popular/trending programs and universities, ensuring every visitor sees engaging content.

🧠 4. The Four Recommendation Modes

The system automatically selects the best scoring strategy for each student based on how much data is available about them. As the student uses the platform more, the system evolves.

🔥
Trending Mode
When: Anonymous visitor OR student with no preferences
Shows programs and universities ranked by overall popularity — how many students have favorited, viewed, or applied to them. This is the default for all visitors who haven't signed up or completed onboarding.
Score = Popularity ÷ 10 × Boosts
🎯
Onboarding Mode
When: Student completed onboarding, but has < 10 interactions
Ranks programs purely based on how well they match the student's stated preferences — degree goal, preferred cities, fields of study, and budget. No behavioral data needed yet.
Score = Onboarding Match × Boosts
👁️
Behavioral Mode
When: Student has ≥ 10 interactions, but NO onboarding preferences
Ranks programs based on the student's actual behavior — what they've clicked on, favorited, applied to, and searched for. Perfect for students who skipped onboarding.
Score = Behavioral Match × Boosts
⚡
Hybrid Mode
When: Student has onboarding preferences AND ≥ 10 interactions
The most intelligent mode. Blends what the student said they want (onboarding) with what they actually do on the platform (behavior). The behavioral weight increases as more data is collected.
Score = (40% × Onboarding + 60% × Behavioral) × Boosts

🔄 Dynamic Weight Shifting: In Hybrid mode, the system starts with a 70% onboarding / 30% behavioral split. Once the student passes 10 interactions, it automatically shifts to 40% onboarding / 60% behavioral — trusting what the student does more than what they initially said.

🚀 7. Smart Boosts — Partner & Freshness

On top of the base score, two multiplicative boosts can push certain programs and universities higher in the rankings.

✅
ScholarBee Partner Boost
+15% score increase
Programs and universities from ScholarBee-verified partner campuses receive an automatic 15% ranking boost. This reflects the higher trust, better data quality, and direct support partnership with these institutions.
Score × 1.15
🟢
Freshness Boost
+10% score increase
Programs with active admission deadlines (deadline is in the future) receive a 10% boost over programs whose deadlines have already passed. This ensures students see actionable, timely opportunities first.
Score × 1.10
Final Score Calculation Example

Example: Student seeking Masters in Computer Science in Islamabad

Program: "MS Computer Science at NUST Islamabad"

✅ Degree match (Masters = Masters) → 0.25 × 1.0 = 0.25
✅ Field match (CS = CS exact) → 0.25 × 1.0 = 0.25
✅ City match (Islamabad = Islamabad) → 0.20 × 1.0 = 0.20
✅ Fee in range (120K in 85K-150K) → 0.15 × 1.0 = 0.15
✅ Marks (default) → 0.10 × 1.0 = 0.10
✅ Timeline (default) → 0.05 × 1.0 = 0.05

Onboarding Score = 1.00 (perfect match!)

✅ ScholarBee Partner → × 1.15
✅ Active Deadline → × 1.10

Final Score = 1.00 × 1.15 × 1.10 = 1.27

📱 8. Where Recommendations Appear

The recommendation system is integrated across all major student-facing pages of the ScholarBee platform.

🏠
Homepage Carousel
The "Explore Top Academic Programs" carousel on the homepage shows the top 15 personalized program recommendations. For logged-in users with preferences, these are sorted by relevance. For anonymous visitors, trending programs are shown.
📚
Programs Listing Page (/programs)
When a logged-in student visits the Programs page without any filters, they see their personalized program recommendations instead of the default chronological list. As soon as they apply any filter (search, city, degree, etc.), the system seamlessly switches to the standard filtered search.
🏛️
Universities Listing Page (/universities)
Similarly, the Universities page shows personalized university recommendations when no filters are active. The universities are ranked by how well they match the student's preferred cities, fields of study, and past interactions.
🎓
Scholarships Page (/search-scholarship)
The scholarships listing continues to use the existing intelligent grouping algorithm which interleaves scholarships from different universities for diversity. Scholarship-specific personalization is planned for a future phase.

🔀 9. Smart Filter/Recommendation Switching

The Programs and Universities pages intelligently switch between two modes to provide the best experience.

🎯
Recommendation Mode
Active when: No filters applied
Student lands on the page with no search or filter criteria. The system shows personalized, ranked recommendations from the AI engine. Results are ordered by relevance score, not by date.
🔍
Search/Filter Mode
Active when: Any filter or search is used
Student applies a search query, selects a city, degree level, or any other filter. The system immediately switches to the standard database search with the applied filters. Recommendations resume when filters are cleared.

✨ Seamless Experience: This switching is completely transparent to the student. They simply see the best possible results at all times — personalized when browsing, filtered when searching. No extra buttons, toggles, or settings required.

🔒 11. Privacy & Data Handling

Student data protection is a core principle of the recommendation system design.

🔐
Data Stays Internal
All recommendation scoring happens on ScholarBee's own servers. No student data is sent to third-party AI services. The scoring engine runs entirely on your backend infrastructure.
👤
Scores Are Private
Students never see their numeric relevance scores. Only administrators can view the raw scores for debugging and tuning purposes. Students simply see a ranked list.
🗑️
Onboarding is Optional
Students can skip the onboarding questionnaire entirely. They can also update their preferences later. The system gracefully degrades to trending mode when no data is available.
📊
Behavioral Data is Aggregated
Only click counts, favorites, and application history are used — no personal browsing history, device fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking is involved.
